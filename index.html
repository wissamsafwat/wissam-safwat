📍 Regional Sales Distribution

Modeled geographic revenue streams across all four territories: West ($764.6K) and East ($611.7K) accounted for ~60% of total volume, while Central ($518.8K) and South ($402.0K) highlighted untapped expansion opportunities.

💡 Category Margin Disparity

Uncovered critical margin health differences: Technology ($145.5K) and Office Supplies ($122.5K) produced the bulk of returns, while Furniture yielded only $18.5K profit despite substantial gross sales.

📈 Monthly Trend & Seasonality

Tracked 48-month time series data showing consistent Q4 revenue acceleration across all years, peaking in November 2019 at $118.4K in single-month sales.
