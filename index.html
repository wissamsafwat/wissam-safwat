📍 Regional Sales Distribution

Modeled geographic revenue streams across all four territories: West ($764.6K) and East ($611.7K) accounted for ~60% of total volume, while Central ($518.8K) and South ($402.0K) highlighted untapped expansion opportunities. Data verified via geographic Pivot aggregation.

💡 Category Margin Disparity

Uncovered critical margin health differences. Technology ($145.5K) and Office Supplies ($122.5K) produced the bulk of returns. Conversely, Furniture yielded only $18.5K in cumulative profit despite substantial gross sales volume, heavily impacting overall margins.

📈 Monthly Trend & Seasonality

Tracked 48-month time series data showing consistent Q4 revenue acceleration across all years, peaking in late Q4 of 2019 at over $118K in single-month sales. This seasonal insight allows for proactive inventory and marketing resource allocation.

Analytical Takeaway: Profit Bottlenecks

Based on the calculated totals in the pivot architecture, Furniture generated heavy order volume but resulted in a fractional 6.4% share of the $286.4K total profit. Hypothesis: High logistical overhead and aggressive discounting mechanisms are eroding margins in this category. Reallocating sales focus toward Technology and high-value Consumer cohorts represents the single largest margin lever validated by this dataset.
