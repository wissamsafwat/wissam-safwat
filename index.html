📍 Balanced Admission Distribution

Analyzed admission channels, revealing a highly stable and balanced distribution: Emergency (33.61%), Urgent (33.46%), and Elective (32.93%). This indicates consistent utilization across all hospital entry points without disproportionate strain on emergency services.

💡 Insurance Revenue Parity

Evaluated financial contribution by insurance provider. Revenue is highly distributed across the top 5 providers, with Cigna leading at $284M, closely followed by Medicare ($283M), Blue Cross ($280M), UnitedHealthcare ($280M), and Aetna ($276M).

📈 Patient Condition Tracking

Visualized primary patient conditions to allocate specialized resources. Arthritis, Diabetes, Hypertension, Obesity, Cancer, and Asthma show almost identical prevalence rates, tracking steadily between 9.1K and 9.2K patients each.

Analytical Takeaway: Operational Stability

The facility exhibits exceptional operational stability. The even distribution of admission types (Elective, Urgent, Emergency) and patient conditions (all hovering around ~9.1K cases) combined with an average length of stay of 15.5 days allows administrators to forecast staffing, bed allocation, and inventory needs with high predictability.

Enterprise HR Attrition & Workforce Analytics

Project Context: A comprehensive Human Resources analytics dashboard designed to audit workforce demographics, evaluate departmental retention, and identify primary drivers of employee turnover across the organization.

My Responsibilities: I modeled a corporate dataset comprising over 1,400 employees to investigate attrition trends. I engineered a dashboard featuring age group distributions, salary slab impacts, and departmental breakdowns to provide leadership with clear visibility into where and why talent is leaving.

📍 Departmental Turnover

Segmented attrition by organizational departments. The data shows distinct turnover volume differences, with the highest concentration of departing employees originating from the R&D/Operations and IT sectors.

💡 Salary Slab Attrition Risk

Cross-referenced salary brackets against attrition probability. While the "6-10 LPA" slab holds the highest total employee count, the lower salary brackets (0-3 LPA and 3-6 LPA) show notable proportional attrition risks that require targeted retention budgets.

📈 Demographics & Gender

The workforce skews heavily toward the 26-35 age group (588 employees) and 36-45 age group (446 employees). Analysis by gender indicates that males account for 63% of the total attrition count (146 employees) compared to 37% for females (85 employees).

Analytical Takeaway: Targeted Retention Efforts

With an organizational attrition rate of 16.3% and 231 employees lost, blanket retention strategies are inefficient. The data strongly suggests focusing HR interventions and compensation reviews on early-to-mid career male employees (ages 26-35) within specific high-turnover technical and operational departments to maximize retention ROI.

My Responsibilities: I executed a full-funnel business intelligence audit across multi-year transactional records (2016–2019). 📍 Regional Sales Distribution

Modeled geographic revenue streams across all four territories: West ($764.6K) and East ($611.7K) accounted for ~60% of total volume, while Central ($518.8K) and South ($402.0K) highlighted untapped expansion opportunities. Data verified via geographic Pivot aggregation.

💡 Category Margin Disparity

Uncovered critical margin health differences. Technology ($145.5K) and Office Supplies ($122.5K) produced the bulk of returns. Conversely, Furniture yielded only $18.5K in cumulative profit despite substantial gross sales volume, heavily impacting overall margins.

📈 Monthly Trend & Seasonality

Tracked 48-month time series data showing consistent Q4 revenue acceleration across all years, peaking in late Q4 of 2019 at over $118K in single-month sales. This seasonal insight allows for proactive inventory and marketing resource allocation.

Analytical Takeaway: Profit Bottlenecks

Based on the calculated totals in the pivot architecture, Furniture generated heavy order volume but resulted in a fractional 6.4% share of the $286.4K total profit. Hypothesis: High logistical overhead and aggressive discounting mechanisms are eroding margins in this category. Reallocating sales focus toward Technology and high-value Consumer cohorts represents the single largest margin lever validated by this dataset.
